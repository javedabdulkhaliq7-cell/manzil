Privacy Policy

IQRA — Empowering Balochistan Through Education

Effective: September 2026

Who we are

IQRA is an exam-preparation app built for Balochistan Board students (Class 9–12 and MDCAT). This policy explains what information we collect when you use IQRA, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used to sign you in, whether through Google Sign-In or a one-time email code.
  • Name and district — optional, added by you after signup, used to personalize your profile and show you on district/class leaderboards.
  • If you sign in with Google: your name and profile picture, as provided by Google — we never see or store your Google password.
  • Class and board selection — which class and exam board you're studying for, so we show you the right content.

As you use the app, we also record:

  • Quiz and mock test attempts, scores, and answers
  • Study progress per chapter (completion, best scores)
  • XP, streaks, and daily usage limits (e.g. how many MCQs or AI Tutor questions you've used today)

How we use this information

  • To run your account and remember your progress across sessions
  • To personalize content to your class and board
  • To show district and class rankings (your name and district appear on the leaderboard, if you've set them — you can leave these blank if you'd rather not appear there)
  • To send you your sign-in code by email
  • To improve IQRA based on how students actually use it

We do not sell your data to advertisers, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Services we use to run IQRA

IQRA is built on a small number of trusted infrastructure providers, who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles authentication.
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Resend — delivers the sign-in code email to your inbox.

None of these providers use your IQRA data for their own advertising.

WhatsApp

IQRA offers an optional WhatsApp Channel for exam alerts and scholarship news, and a WhatsApp contact for Premium upgrades. Joining the Channel or messaging us on WhatsApp is entirely your choice — we never collect your phone number through the app itself, and WhatsApp's own privacy policy governs anything you share there directly.

Children's privacy

IQRA is built for students, many of whom are under 18. We only collect what's needed to run the app (listed above) and never ask for sensitive personal information beyond that. If you're a parent or guardian with questions about your child's account, contact us using the details below.

How long we keep your data

We keep your account and progress data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and data deleted, contact us and we'll remove it.

Your rights

  • You can edit your name and district anytime from your Profile.
  • You can request a copy of your data.
  • You can request your account be deleted entirely.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of IQRA after a change means you accept the updated policy.
